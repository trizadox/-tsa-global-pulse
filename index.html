Where the data comes from

Every number on this page is fetched live, in your browser, from the World Bank Open Data Indicators API (World Development Indicators and related sources). Many health figures in it come from WHO, UN agencies and national statistics offices — the original source of each indicator is listed below.

Reading the numbers carefully

Most indicators are updated once a year, and the latest year is often incomplete because countries report at different times. The year slider starts on the most recent year that most countries have reported. Regional and income groups (e.g. “Sub-Saharan Africa”, “World”) are World Bank aggregates. They are left out of the map and ranking, but you can add them to the Trends chart.
